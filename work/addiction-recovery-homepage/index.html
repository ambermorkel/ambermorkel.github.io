A rehab homepage optimized

Client
Heartfelt Recovery Centers, a US addiction-treatment center
Role
On-page SEO strategy, restructure and copy
Year
2025
Sector
Addiction treatment
Scope
SEO strategy, Copywriting
Deliverables
Keyword research, Homepage restructure and copy refresh, Meta title optimization, Descriptive alt text, Internal links to key service pages
Tools
Google Search Console

690 Organic clicks in 3 months

32,900 Impressions 3 months

Visit the live site

Brief

The homepage of Heartfelt Recovery Centers, a rehab center in the USA, was underperforming in search and not supporting admissions growth.

Approach

Research the keywords tied to addiction treatment services, matched to what searchers actually mean. Restructure the page: header hierarchy, keyword placement, internal links, and visible calls to action. Rewrite for patients: accessible and conversion-minded, without losing clarity or trust.

Execution

I restructured the homepage on SEO best practices and refreshed the copy. I tightened the on-page fundamentals too: the meta title, descriptive alt text, and internal link signals to key service pages.

Result

Over 3 months, the homepage earned 690 clicks and 32,900 impressions from organic search. The client reported a rise in admissions in that period.

  • The homepage work launched on January 12, 2025.
