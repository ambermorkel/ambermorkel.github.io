Brief

Arcara Access, a US women's health practice with a menopause specialty, needed a homepage built from the ground up. It had to introduce the brand, show its services, and invite action.

Approach

Balance service-specific search terms with approachable, brand-aligned language. Structure the page in clear sections: the brand, service highlights, differentiators, a way into each service page. Write for patients and referral sources alike.

Execution

I led the build: keyword research first, then the content framework and outline, then the final copy.

Result

In the first month, the homepage recorded 79 clicks and 2,050 impressions, a 3.9% click-through rate and an average position of 10.

  • The homepage launched on March 18, 2025.
