Selected work / Case study

50 calls and 12 admissions in a month

Client
Aftermath Behavioral Health, a US mental-health treatment provider
Role
Keyword research and copywriting
Year
2024
Sector
Mental health treatment
Scope
Paid search, Copywriting
Deliverables
Keyword research, PPC landing page copy
Tools
Call Tracking Metrics (CTM)

50 Phone calls from the page in the first month

12 New admissions in the first month

Visit the live site

Brief

Aftermath Behavioral Health needed enrollment in its Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP) for anxiety treatment. The traffic would come from paid search, so the page had to work for someone already looking for help.

Approach

Target the high-intent keywords people use when they search for outpatient anxiety treatment. Write for one reader at one moment: ready to ask for help. Make the next step, a phone call, the obvious one.

Execution

I ran the keyword research and wrote the page. It was designed specifically for paid search traffic and launched on November 4, 2024.

Result

In the first month, the page generated 50 phone calls and 12 new admissions in the program.
