One honest question, 148,000 impressions

Visit the live site

Brief

Aftermath Treatment Center wanted to meet people early, when they are still asking whether they can stop drinking on their own. The question is common, emotionally loaded, and competitive.

Approach

Answer a high-intent query honestly, including the risk. Serve readers at the early stage of seeking help, before any treatment decision. Link onward to the treatment program pages.

Execution

I handled the keyword strategy and wrote "Alcohol Withdrawal: Is It Safe to Quit Drinking Cold Turkey?", published on September 16, 2024.

Result

Over 6 months, the blog earned 2,740 clicks and 148,000 impressions, at a 1.9% average click-through rate and an average position of 21.7.
